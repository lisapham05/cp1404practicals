## Estimates

Regarding the **estimates** that you did for practical tasks...

### How was your estimate accuracy usually?

Normally I estimate around 30 minutes for a code exercise. Sometimes it took me longer, something shorter. 
The estimate accuracy is around 50%

### How did your estimate accuracy improve or change during the course of the subject?

It is improving. It used to take me longer to do the code, however, as I practice more, I am get used to it.
So the accuracy is improving

### What did you learn from doing these estimates?

Estimation is a critical skill for project management that extends beyond just writing code.
I also learned that the majority of time is often spent not on the initial implementation, but on debugging, testing, 
and refinement—something I now explicitly account for in any planning.

## Code Reviews

### What have you learned from being reviewed by other people?

The feedback I received highlighted areas where my variable names were ambiguous or my logic was too convoluted. 
It also exposed me to alternative—and often more efficient—ways of solving the same problem, 
which broadened my own problem-solving toolkit.

### What have you learned from doing code reviews of other people?

It trained me to read code with a critical eye for quality, looking for things like "magic numbers," 
repeated code, and adherence to style guides, which in turn has made my own code cleaner and more maintainable.
